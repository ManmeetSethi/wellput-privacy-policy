REJOINDER

Privacy Policy

Effective: 13 August 2026

Rejoinder is an iOS app that helps you draft replies to workplace messages. You can paste a message or upload a screenshot, add context, and receive AI-generated reply options. Rejoinder creates an anonymous account identifier, so you can use the app without providing your name or email address. Rejoinder is operated by Manmeet Sethi as an individual developer. For privacy questions or data requests, email m20sethi@gmail.com.

2. How We Use Data

  • Generate, refine, display, copy, and store the workplace replies you request.
  • Maintain your cloud history and sync your Rejoinder data.
  • Activate, restore, and reconcile subscription access.
  • Protect the service from abuse and investigate reliability or security problems.
  • Understand how the product is used and improve its flows, prompts, and generated results.
  • Respond to support, privacy, and deletion requests.

3. AI Processing

The message, screenshot, context, and instructions you submit are processed on our servers and sent through OpenRouter to a third-party AI model provider to generate the requested reply. We store the model request and response, including refinement rounds, prior generations, and the prompt version used, so the result can be displayed, refined, supported, and evaluated. Do not submit confidential, personal, or sensitive information that you are not permitted to share.

4. Analytics and Session Replay

Rejoinder uses product analytics and session replay to understand the complete app experience. Those records may include workplace text, screenshots, context, generated replies, and the app screens and interactions shown during a session. Text and images are not masked in these product recordings. We do not use this information for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

6. Payments

Apple processes payments, and RevenueCat helps us verify subscription access. Rejoinder does not receive or store your full payment-card or bank-account details. Apple purchase records remain subject to Apple's account and retention rules, including after you delete Rejoinder data.

7. Sharing and Sale of Data

We share data with the service providers above only as needed to operate, secure, support, and improve Rejoinder, or when required by law. We do not sell personal data. We do not use Rejoinder data for third-party advertising or tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

8. Retention and Deletion

Submitted content, generated replies, and related product records remain available until you delete a situation, clear your history, or delete your account from Rejoinder Settings. Account deletion starts removal of your Rejoinder content and account data. Limited records may remain where needed for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, subscription reconciliation, dispute resolution, or provider backup cycles.

9. Security

We use encrypted network connections, owner-scoped cloud records, app-integrity checks, and access controls designed to protect Rejoinder data. No transmission or storage method can guarantee absolute security.

10. Your Choices and Rights

  • Delete individual situations, clear your history, or delete your account and product data in Rejoinder Settings.
  • Request access, correction, or deletion by emailing m20sethi@gmail.com.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription through your Apple account. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data.

11. Children's Privacy

Rejoinder is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13, or under the minimum age required by applicable law in their location.

12. Changes and Contact

We may update this policy when Rejoinder or applicable requirements change. The current policy will remain published here with its effective date. For privacy questions or data requests, email m20sethi@gmail.com.
